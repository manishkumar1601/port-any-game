# Scope and contribution bar

port-any-game ports games the user owns to the browser. It does not provide game data, cracks, or DRM circumvention.

## What we accept

A bug report or change must describe a real porting failure. Include:

- **Game type**: engine or runtime family (for example a Unity WebGL export, an NW.js game, a DOS game).
- **Lane**: which porting lane the skill chose.
- **Phase**: survey, lane selection, build, pack, serve or verify.
- **Observed vs expected**: what happened, and what should have happened.

Reports without a concrete failure (feature wishes, speculative lanes, support for a title with no reproduced problem) are closed until they have one.

## What we do not accept

- Game data, game-data URLs or anything copyrighted in the repo, fixtures or catalog.
- GPL-licensed code vendored into the plugin.
- Telemetry of any kind.
